Care guide

Looking after Mochi

  • Pet

    Drag a finger across the kitty. Hearts, purrs and happy eyes follow.

  • Feed & brush

    Keep the four little meters happy. A grubby cat gets dusty and a hungry cat gets grumpy.

  • Tilt toy

    Tap Tilt and move your phone to roll the ball. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for motion permission first. No sensor? Drag the ball instead.

  • Naps

    Naps refill energy while the room dims. Tap Wake whenever you're ready to play.
