How to hold the line

Command

  • Tap a friendly unit to select it.
  • Tap a teal tile to move. Tap a red target to fire.
  • Drag the field to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Hold ends a unit's turn without acting.

Take ground

  • Move onto a city to capture it. Cities pay funds every turn.
  • Infantry are cheap and fast through rough ground. Battle tanks hit hard.
  • Artillery fires at range 2–3 and never takes a counterattack.
  • Capture the enemy HQ to win. Lose yours and the sector falls.

Tilt controls

  • Tap Tilt to pan the camera by moving your device.
  • On iOS, motion access must be granted in the prompt.
  • Tap Tilt again to switch it off and recalibrate.

Field notes

  • Terrain raises defence — forests, hills and cities.
  • Units on friendly cities repair 25 armour at turn start.
  • Direct-fire units counterattack. Artillery does not.
