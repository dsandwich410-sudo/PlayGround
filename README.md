## GRIDFRONT

A touch-first 32×32 tactical war game. Two command posts, twelve contested cities, four unit types, and fog of war. Capture the coalition HQ before they capture yours.

**Play:** run `./start.sh` and open the served URL (port 3000 by default).

**Controls**

- Tap a blue unit, then a highlighted tile to move. Tap a marked red unit in range to fire.
- Drag to pan, pinch or use the wheel to zoom. The minimap jumps the camera.
- Tap an owned, empty city to reinforce. End a move on a city to capture it.
- On phones, enable **Tilt Controls** to pan by leaning (iOS prompts for motion access).
- Keyboard: `E`/`Enter` end turn, `W` wait, `R` recenter, `T` tilt, `M` mute, arrows pan, `+`/`-` zoom, `Esc` deselect or close a menu.
